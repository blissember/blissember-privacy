Privacy Policy

App: Couples Board Game: Dice　|　Effective date: 2026-09-01

In short

We collect no data at all. This app runs entirely offline. There is no server, no account, no advertising, and no third-party analytics. Everything you create stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, upload, share, or sell any personal information.

  • No registration, no sign-in
  • No name, email, phone number, date of birth, or location requested
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA)

2. Where your data lives

Your game progress, player settings, and custom content are stored locally on your device using on-device storage (localStorage). They never leave your device.

They will be removed if you clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or if the system reclaims storage. We cannot restore them for you. Use the in-app "Export" feature to keep your own backup.

3. Export and import

The in-app "Export / Import" feature copies and pastes text entirely on your own device. Nothing is transmitted to us — we operate no servers.

4. Network activity

The app makes zero network requests. It is a single bundled HTML file loaded directly from the app package through the system web view. It contains no fetch, XMLHttpRequest, or WebSocket calls, and loads no external resources or third-party SDKs.

5. Purchases (content unlock)

The app offers three one-time in-app purchases — a 7-day pass ($2.99), a 30-day pass ($4.99), and a lifetime unlock ($19.99) — each of which unlocks the full content library for the corresponding period (or permanently, for the lifetime option). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store:

  • We never receive or store your payment details
  • We cannot see your Apple ID or any identifying information
  • Unlock status is recorded on your device only; use "Restore Purchases" after switching devices
  • Time-limited passes expire automatically; when one expires the app simply returns to the free tier, and no data is deleted

6. Third-party services

None. There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social integrations, and no push notification services.

7. Children's privacy

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. Because we collect no data at all, no such information can be stored.

8. Data security

Because all data remains on your device, protecting it comes down to securing the device itself: use a passcode and avoid sharing your device with others while the app is open.

9. Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Since we hold no contact information, we cannot notify you directly.
