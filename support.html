Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock all the content?

Open the Unlock tab and choose one of three one-time options:

  • $2.99 — 7-day pass. Everything unlocked for 7 days. Custom decks are not included.
  • $4.99 — 30-day pass. Everything unlocked for 30 days, including custom decks.
  • $19.99 — Lifetime. Everything unlocked forever, including custom decks.

After a timed pass expires, the app returns to the free tier. Your purchased content is not deleted — buying another pass restores full access immediately.

I paid but the content is still locked. What should I do?

  1. Open the Unlock tab and tap Restore Purchases.
  2. There is a second Restore Purchases button on the About tab — either one works.
  3. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.
  4. If it still does not unlock, email us with your purchase date and we will sort it out.

I switched to a new iPhone. Do I have to pay again?

No. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not your device. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore Purchases.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, completely. The app runs entirely on your device and makes zero network requests. Airplane mode changes nothing.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no advertising. Your game progress and custom content are stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Where is my game progress stored? Will I lose it?

Progress, player names, and custom decks are stored locally on your device. They will be erased if you delete the app, clear its data, or reset the device. We cannot recover them, because we never receive them. Use the in-app Export feature to keep your own backup.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. This app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It contains mature, suggestive content that is not suitable for minors.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. Refund requests are handled by Apple, not by us: open reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and select the purchase.

The timed pass expired sooner than I expected.

Passes are counted from the moment of purchase, using your device clock. If you believe the timer is wrong, email us with the purchase date and time from your App Store receipt and we will investigate.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We would love to hear it. Email shigechenbi@foxmail.com with a short description, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Screenshots help a lot.
